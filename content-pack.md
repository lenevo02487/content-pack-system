## Pack ke peeche ki soch

- **Rhythm:** hafte me 7-8 posts — 3 reels, baaki photo/carousel, taaki feed bhaari na lage aur reach bhi bane.
- **Sthaniya rang:** Digboi ki heritage, Assam ke bagaan, kulhad aur adda — wahi cheezein jo is cafe ko shehar ke baaki cafes se alag karti hain.
- **Har post ek kaam:** kuch posts bharosa banate hain (kahani/testimonial), kuch bikri (offers), kuch baat-cheet badhate hain (vote/sawal) — sirf photo daalna maksad nahi.
- **Samay:** subah ke posts chai ke time pe (7-8:30 AM), reels shaam ke peak pe (5:30-6:30 PM) — jab log asal me phone pe hote hain.
